# FitnessHub Analytics Project
### Customer Cohort Analysis
Customers tend to cancel their subscriptions after roughly the 2 month mark. This is supported by the most significant dropoff in active customers between month 2 and month 3.
### Revenue Forecast
Expected subscription revenue over the next 12 months is forecasted to be about $320k.
### CAC vs. LTV
It takes about 5 months for the 2024 cohort to recover customer acquisition cost and 10 months for the 2025 cohort.
